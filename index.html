FROM NOMINATION TO FIREWORKS

A whole election,
without leaving town.

Start one when your town is ready, or let the plugin schedule them for you. Residents can vote from a simple GUI or straight from chat.

YOUR TOWN HALL/election
  1. 01

    Open nominations

    Residents run for office, set a campaign message, and can organize into parties.

    /election run
  2. 02

    Cast a real ballot

    Town residents vote in the GUI or with a command. Changeable or secret ballots are configurable.

    /election vote
  3. 03

    Count every voice

    Plurality, instant-runoff ranked choice, or approval voting. Tie-breakers are yours to choose.

    YOUR RULES
  4. 04

    Celebrate the winner

    Reveal the results in stages, then hand over configured ranks or mayorship automatically.

THE ORIGIN STORY

One pull request.
A whole new chapter.

↗ GOOD IDEAS
GET A RECOUNT

I found a cool Minecraft project that brought elections to Towny servers. I liked the idea, so I made a pull request to help improve it.

Turns out, its creator had abandoned the project. Somehow, my pull request reminded them it was still there — and they decided to archive it soon after. I noticed the next day.

I didn't want a good idea to disappear, so I started my own fork and used AI as a coding partner to bring it forward. That fork became Towny Elections Reborn.
